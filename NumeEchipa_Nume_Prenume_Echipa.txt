=======================================================
ARHITECTURA TEHNICA
=======================================================

- Oracle Cloud ATP Free Tier (2 instante, aceeasi regiune: eu-turin-1)
- ATP1 (bankdb): nodul Central — GLOBAL_USER
- ATP2 (globalbanklocal): nodurile Locale — BUCHAREST_USER + CLUJ_USER
- Comunicare inter-nod: DB Links cu DBMS_CLOUD_ADMIN (TCPS/mTLS)
- Front-end: Flask + python-oracledb thin mode (fara Oracle Client)
- Fragmente: 2 orizontale (CONTURI, TRANZACTII) + 1 verticala (CLIENTI)
- Replicare: TIPURI_CONT (master pe ATP1, slave pe ATP2)
